    Rules & regulations

    Read each section, then confirm every statement below.

    Course duration & access
    • The course duration is six months, according to the selected batch.
    • Course access is strictly personal and non-transferable.
    • Sharing login credentials or admission access is prohibited.
    Online classes & recorded video restrictions
    • Unauthorised downloading, screen recording, copying or redistribution of recorded lectures is prohibited.
    • Sharing videos, screenshots, PDFs, question banks or study materials with non-enrolled individuals is not permitted.
    • Uploading or selling academy materials on other platforms is prohibited.
    • All course resources are for personal educational use only.
    Offline classroom rules
    • Students must maintain punctuality and follow the classroom timetable.
    • Respectful behaviour towards faculty, staff and fellow students is expected.
    • Unauthorised recording, photography or distribution of classroom lectures and materials is prohibited.
    • Students must maintain classroom discipline.
    Violation of academy policies
    • Confirmed unauthorised sharing, distribution or misuse of course resources may result in disciplinary action, including suspension or termination of course access.
    • The academy may investigate reported violations and give the student an opportunity to respond.
    • Refund consequences will follow the published policy and applicable law.
    Academic responsibilities
    • Students are expected to attend classes regularly and follow the study schedule.
    • Assigned tests and practice questions should be completed.
    • Doubts should be raised through designated academy channels.
    • Online students are responsible for maintaining suitable internet access and devices.
    Communication & conduct
    • Students must maintain respectful communication with faculty and fellow learners.
    • Harassment, abusive communication and deliberate disruption are prohibited.
    • Official announcements will be communicated through academy channels.
    Fees & refund policy
    • Students must pay the agreed course fee according to the selected payment plan.
    • Instalment arrangements must be confirmed by the academy.
    • Refund eligibility and conditions will follow the refund policy communicated before enrolment.
    Intellectual property
    • Original lectures, notes, presentations, question banks and academy-created resources are protected by applicable intellectual property rights.
    • Materials are provided for personal educational purposes only.
    • Unauthorised commercial use or distribution is prohibited.
    Final agreement
    • By submitting this application, the student confirms that they have read and understood the academy’s Rules & Regulations.
    • The academy may take appropriate action for violations in accordance with published terms and applicable law.
    Privacy notice

    The details and files you submit are stored securely by Spaceman Academy for Life Sciences and used only for admission administration and course-related communication. We don’t sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. You can ask the academy to correct or delete your information at any time.
